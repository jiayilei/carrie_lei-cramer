About Me

I am working towards my Ph.D. in Statistics at Texas A&M University, co-advised by Dr. Huiyan Sang and Dr. Matthias Katzfuss. My research focuses on uncertainty quantification, Gaussian processes, and the intersection of statistics with ML/AI.

I completed my B.S. in Applied Mathematics (computation track) with a minor in Computer Science at the University of California, Davis. Afterward, I worked as an analyst at AAA NCNU. During my Ph.D., I interned at NASA JPL, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, and American Express.
